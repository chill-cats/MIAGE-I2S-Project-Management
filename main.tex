\section{Introduction to project}

To improve student success, engagement, and learning experience, the university's
executive board has launched an initiative to develop an AI-powered Academic
Assistant available through web and mobile platforms.

The assistant should help students throughout their academic journey by providing
services such as study planning, exam preparation support, resource
recommendations, course information, deadline reminders, and personalized learning
guidance.

The solution must be integrated with existing university systems while
ensuring compliance with privacy and data protection requirements.
Your team acts as the product and project team responsible for defining, planning, and
managing the development and launch of the new AI Academic Assistant.
